Why partner

Campaigns that pay for buying and holding can attract people who leave when the payout ends. A club changes the reason to stay: your friends can see whether you showed up this week. Tropa Club measures that difference openly, on chain data, with a method published before the results (see Retention evidence). We sell the measured retention, not the clicks.

The pilot

A qualifying frontend for your existing campaign

You already run a campaign with rules, for example a reward for buying a stock token and holding it for a set time. In the pilot, Tropa Club becomes a qualifying frontend for that campaign: a purchase made through a club counts under your existing rules.

  • You get attributable flow. Every club buy carries Builder Code bc_vdp89s9b and you can verify the count on chain yourself.
  • Your users keep their rewards. We do not take a cut of your campaign and we do not replace it.
  • You get a retention read. We report the D+30 hold rate of club members against a cohort of your own non-club buyers, matched the same way as our public method.

The margin that counts as beating your cohort is written into the pilot terms before it starts. Pricing is flat, in tiers by active club members: people who open the app or check in, never the number of buyers.

What we never do

  • We never hold perk funds. If a perk is paid to members, the partner pays it through its own campaign or tool. Tropa Club publishes an eligibility list.
  • We never rank members for a perk. A perk is equal for every eligible member, and there is always a free Bench path.
  • We never show a partner perk before it is signed. Nothing on the site implies a partnership that does not exist.
  • No prize depends on returns. See the rule.
  • We state what qualifies. At the buy step the app says plainly whether a campaign’s rewards apply to this purchase. It says so when they do not.

White-label clubs

The club engine, with lineups, Matchday cards, streaks, call-ups and fixtures, can run under a partner’s own name and crest, with the partner’s attribution on every buy. The same rules apply: non-custodial, no advice, no prize on returns, and the buy flow hidden where it cannot be offered.

White-label clubs are not on the launch list. We scope them with a partner after a pilot. If you want them, say so when you get in touch.
