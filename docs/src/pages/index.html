Tropa Club does two things. It counts who really holds each Coinbase stock token on Base, and from Tue Nov 24, 14:30 UTC it runs free clubs where friends each buy about $10 of stock tokens a week from their own wallets, with the streaks and everyone’s cost basis kept for them.

Real holders of every stock token. Clubs for your group chat.

What Tropa Club is

Tropa Club is a free app for friends who each want to buy stock tokens from their own wallets, and keep each other going, without a brokerage in the middle. A club is a group chat’s worth of people who each buy about $10 of stock tokens a week, every purchase signed from the member’s own wallet. There is no shared pot. Tropa Club never holds the money. It finds the route, checks the price against a guard, tags the transaction with a Builder Code so Base can count it, and records who bought. It charges no fee.

The name is everyday speech in Brazil, Mexico and the Philippines: “join my tropa” means “join my crew”. Some words in the product come from football. The glossary below says what each one means.

A stock token tracks the price of a share. You do not own the share: there is no vote and no claim on the company. Coinbase’s tokens are issued on Base by a Coinbase special-purpose company (an SPV, a company set up only to issue and back them) and are described as an onchain certificate that provides economic exposure. Tropa Club calls them “stock tokens” everywhere and never “shares”. See what you hold.

How it works

Seven ideas make up the whole product. Each has a page section that gives the exact rule.

Clubs

A private club is your group chat. A pick-up club puts people without a group chat together, matched by country first and then by region, and counts from three members. Each stock token also has an open club, such as the NVIDIA open club, which anyone can join for free and which is ranked on its own. Club types

Played and Bench

A gameweek is one trading week. You played it if you bought ${{playUsd}} or more through Tropa Club inside it. About ${{habitUsd}} a week is the habit. Members who have not bought yet this gameweek are on the Bench. Joining a club is free and needs no buy. The lineup rule

Matchday card

Your club’s weekly card: who has bought this gameweek, who hasn’t, and the turnout. It is refreshed after each US trading day closes. Gameweeks and the Matchday card

Fixtures

From week 1 (Mon Nov 30), private and pick-up clubs of 3 or more are paired each week with a club of a similar size. {{fixtureShort}} Any club where 80% or more of members bought gets 1 bonus point. Returns decide nothing. Bragging rights only. Fixtures

Nudges (call-ups)

A member who has bought this gameweek can tap a teammate on the Bench. That opens a ready-made WhatsApp or Telegram message: “we need you for the 80% bonus this week”. One nudge per teammate per gameweek, and no prize attached. Nudges

Open club rankings

Open clubs are ranked on how many members bought this gameweek and how many are on a streak. No returns appear in the ranking. Open club rankings

Streaks

A streak is a habit badge: buy ${{playUsd}} or more through Tropa Club at least once every 14 days, with one free skip a calendar month (a gap of up to 28 days). Streak

Your first week

  1. Connect a wallet.A Base Account, or a browser wallet like MetaMask or Rabby. Tropa Club only asks it to sign the approval and the buy you choose.
  2. Join or start a club.Start a club and send the invite link to your group chat, or ask to be matched into a pick-up club if you are on your own. Joining is free.
  3. Buy ${{playUsd}} or more (about ${{habitUsd}} is the habit).Pay in {{payWith}}, pick a stock token, and review the route and the 1.5% guard before you sign. See Buying and safety.
  4. Watch the card.Your club’s Matchday card shows who has bought this week. Tap a friend on the Bench to send them a nudge.

Rules we keep

  • Non-custodial, 0% fee (we never hold your money). No Tropa Club contract holds your funds. See Buying and safety.
  • No advice. Nothing in the product or these docs says what to buy, sell or hold.
  • Never ranked by size. Seats, ranks and tables use time, consistency and participation, not how much you hold.
  • No prize tied to returns. Returns can appear as a status. Nothing of value depends on them.
  • Not available to US persons or in sanctioned regions. The buy flow and every club button are hidden in the US and its territories, sanctioned countries, Russia and Belarus, and on Tor; buying is refused when the country cannot be told. Read-only pages stay open everywhere. See Compliance and limits.
