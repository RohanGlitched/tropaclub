What Tropa Club is

Tropa Club is a free app for friends who want to invest together without a brokerage in the middle. A club is a group chat’s worth of people who each buy about $10 of stock tokens a week, every purchase signed from the member’s own wallet. Tropa Club never holds the money. It finds the route, checks the price against a guard, tags the transaction with a Builder Code so Base can count it, and records who showed up. It charges no fee.

The name is everyday speech in Brazil, Mexico and the Philippines: “join my tropa” means “join my crew”. The vocabulary of the product comes from football: Matchday, fixtures, call-ups, the Bench, pennants.

A stock token tracks the price of a share. You do not own the share: there is no vote and no claim on the company. Coinbase’s tokens are issued on Base by a Coinbase SPV and are described as an onchain certificate that provides economic exposure. Tropa Club calls them “stock tokens” everywhere and never “shares”. How it works

Seven ideas make up the whole product. Each has a page section that gives the exact rule.

Clubs

A private club is your group chat. A pick-up club seats solo joiners together, matched by country first and then by region, and plays from three members. A public stock club exists for each stock token and is ranked on a separate table. Club types

Playing and Bench

You play a gameweek by buying at least $10 inside it. Everyone else on the team sheet is on the Bench. The Bench is free and earns nothing: it is a seat on the sheet. The lineup rule

Matchday

Each NYSE trading day closes with a standings card for your club: who played, who is on the Bench, and the top scorer of the week so far. Gameweeks and Matchdays

Fixtures

From GW1 (Mon Nov 30), private and pick-up clubs are paired by size and play one fixture a gameweek: win 3, draw 1, loss 0, plus 1 for at least 80% of members playing. Fixtures

Call-ups

A playing member can tap a benched teammate. That opens a ready-made WhatsApp or Telegram message: “we need you for the 80% bonus this week”. One tap per teammate per gameweek, and no prize attached. Call-ups

Supporters table

Public stock clubs are ranked on participation and streaks only. No returns appear on it. Supporters table

Streaks

The payday streak is a habit badge: one buy of at least $10 in any rolling 14 days keeps it alive, with one free skip a month. Payday streak

Your first Matchday

  1. Connect a wallet.A Base Account or any injected browser wallet. Tropa Club only asks it to sign the approval and the buy you choose.
  2. Join or start a club.Send the invite link to your group chat, or opt in to a pick-up club if you are on your own.
  3. Buy $10 or more.Pay in USDC, pick a stock token, and review the route and the 1.5% guard before you sign. See Buying and safety.
  4. Watch the card.Your club’s Matchday card shows who has played. Tap a benched friend to call them up.

Rules we keep

  • Non-custodial, 0% fee. No Tropa Club contract holds your funds. See Buying and safety.
  • No advice. Nothing in the product or these docs says what to buy, sell or hold.
  • Never ranked by size. Seats, ranks and tables use time, consistency and participation, not how much you hold.
  • No prize tied to returns. Returns can appear as a status. Nothing of value depends on them.
  • Not available to US persons. The buy flow and every club call-to-action are hidden in the US and its territories. Read-only pages stay open everywhere. See Compliance and limits.
