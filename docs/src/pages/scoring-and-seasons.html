Tropa Club keeps score in gameweeks: trading weeks that follow the New York Stock Exchange calendar. You played a gameweek if you bought ${{playUsd}} or more through Tropa Club inside it. Clubs are scored on how many members bought, with one fixture a week from week 1 (GW1, Mon Nov 30).

How pick-up matching works

  • Matching starts inside your country, widens to your region after 24 hours in the queue, and to the whole world after 48 hours.
  • Clubs get a region name and a generated crest. A club never shows a member’s country, and the queue keeps only a salted hash of it, deleted once you are matched.
  • Two wallets with the same first USDC or WETH sender are never put in the same club.
  • You can opt out of matching at any time.

Gameweeks and the Matchday card

A gameweek (GW) is one trading week. It runs from the first NYSE session of the week to the last close of the week. Stock tokens trade around the clock on DEXs, so the rules say precisely which buys belong to which gameweek.

  • Buy window. A buy counts for the gameweek whose window it falls in. A window opens at the previous gameweek’s final close and shuts at its own final close. A buy on Saturday counts for the next gameweek.
  • Matchday card. Your club’s weekly card: who has bought this gameweek, who hasn’t, and the turnout. It is refreshed after each US trading day closes (4:00 pm ET, or 1:00 pm ET on a half-day).
  • GW0 is the practice week. Its window opens on Tue Nov 24 2026 at 00:00 ET, and buying opens on Tue Nov 24, 14:30 UTC. It has a table but no fixtures.
  • Season 0 runs from Nov 24 2026 to the close on Fri Jan 29 2027: GW0 to GW9.

The lineup rule

A member has played a gameweek only after a qualifying buy of ${{playUsd}} or more inside that gameweek’s buy window. Members who have not bought yet this gameweek are on the Bench. The rule is applied to the gameweek, so a buy last week does not count for this week.

What counts as a qualifying buy

A buy qualifies when all of these hold:

  • it was made through Tropa Club, so it carries the Builder Code tag;
  • it is worth at least ${{playUsd}} at the time of the buy;
  • it is not part of a wash pattern: a buy, then a sell, then another buy of the same token within 24 hours scores nothing, for either buy;
  • the wallet is not on the public exclusion list (founder and test wallets).

What the Matchday card shows

  • Only members who bought this gameweek count toward a fixture result and toward the 80% bonus point.
  • Each Matchday card shows who has bought, who is on the Bench and a top scorer line: the member who bought with the best gameweek return so far, shown as a percentage.
  • Joining a club is free and needs no buy. Being on the Bench only means you have not bought yet this gameweek. It never earns a perk or a prize.

Member return and the club score

A member’s gameweek return is the time-weighted percentage return of the stock tokens they bought through Tropa Club, measured over the gameweek’s window. Lots are matched first in, first out, and valued at the token’s mark at the cut: the Chainlink round at the cut, or else the stored DEX marks. A return is a percentage, never a dollar amount.

A club’s Matchday card also shows the median return of the members who bought that gameweek, as a status only. It decides no fixture and no table: those go by turnout (below). Open clubs show no return.

Fixtures

A fixture is a weekly pairing. Fixtures start in GW1 (Mon Nov 30 2026). Each week, private and pick-up clubs that have at least three members when the pairs are made are paired with a club of a similar size. {{fixtureShort}} Turnout is the share of a club’s members who bought $5 or more through Tropa Club that week. It is for bragging rights only: no prize depends on it, and returns decide nothing. Open clubs are never paired.

Pairing

Clubs are grouped into size bands of 3 to 5, 6 to 10, 11 to 20 and 21 or more members. Within a band the pairing is a shuffle seeded by the gameweek and the band, so it is reproducible. If a band has an odd club, it moves up into the next band. The last club left gets a bye.

Nudges (call-ups)

A member who has bought this gameweek can tap a teammate on the Bench on the Matchday card. That opens a ready-made WhatsApp or Telegram message: “we need you for the 80% bonus this week”. It is a nudge between friends and carries no prize. Limits: one nudge per teammate per gameweek from each sender, and nudges are hidden wherever buying is unavailable.

Open club rankings

Open clubs are ranked in their own table (once called the Supporters table) on participation and streaks only. No returns appear on it, and no stock is ranked against another by price.

  1. Participation: the share of the club’s members who bought that gameweek.
  2. Streaks: the share of members with a live payday streak.
  3. Ties go to the club with more members, then by name.

Payday streak

A payday streak is a personal habit badge. It counts paydays: the days you bought, as long as no gap is too long. It does not change fixtures or tables.

  • One qualifying buy of ${{playUsd}} or more through Tropa Club in any rolling 14 days keeps the streak alive.
  • Each calendar month has one free skip: a gap of up to 28 days survives if the month in which the missed 14-day deadline fell has not used its skip.
  • Same-day buys count once.

The streak is there for people who earn irregularly: the Matchday card asks for a buy this gameweek, but the streak only asks for a buy every 14 days or so.

Scouts

Every member has a personal invite link: tropaclub.xyz/j/ followed by a code. Club invite links a member sends carry the same code. A scout is a member who refers friends, and gets a credit only when a friend’s first buy through Tropa Club is still held at ${{scouts.holdUsd}} or more on day 7.

  • First link counts. A new wallet, one with no verified buy and no other club yet, that arrives through the link is linked to that scout once, and the link never changes. You cannot scout yourself.
  • Only real buys count. The friend’s first verified buy through Tropa Club after arriving, still held at ${{scouts.holdUsd}} or more of that token on day 7. Sign-ups and joins earn nothing. (This ${{scouts.holdUsd}} line is not the ${{realFanUsd}} real-fan line.)
  • No credit for wallets you funded. {{scouts.funderRule}} The first sender is the first address that sent the wallet USDC or WETH in the 30 days before the check; exchange and bridge wallets are ignored.
  • Limit. At most {{scouts.creditsPerGw}} credits per scout per gameweek, counted in the gameweek of the friend’s first buy.
  • Stamps. A Scout stamp on the ticket at {{scouts.scoutAt}} credited friend, a Captain’s armband at {{scouts.captainAt}}. The Top scouts table ranks scouts by credited friends who still hold ${{scouts.holdUsd}} or more.
  • First 1,000 stamp. The first {{scouts.foundersLimit}} wallets whose first verified buy was still held at ${{scouts.holdUsd}} or more at D+7 get a “First 1,000” stamp on their ticket. It is separate from the Founders’ Pennant crest below.

No prizes tied to returns

Returns can appear as a status on a Matchday card. Nothing depends on them: no fixture, no table, no prize, rebate, fee or access. Fixtures, the bonus point and every table go by turnout. The brand never uses odds, bets, jackpots or “win” language around money: a fixture is described as more or fewer of a club playing, and the points it takes.

Founders’ Pennant

The Founders’ Pennant is an onchain crest, an attestation on Base through the Ethereum Attestation Service, with no money in it. It goes to every member who bought in GW0 (Nov 24 to Nov 27) and still held the position seven days after their first buy. Every eligible member gets the same crest, with no ranking.

No partner perk is shown here or on the site until one is signed. If a partner perk is agreed, it is paid by the partner through its own tool to an eligibility list we publish, and Tropa Club never holds those funds. Crests for table positions in later gameweeks and seasons are planned, and they carry no money either.
