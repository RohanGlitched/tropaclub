Tropa Club keeps score in gameweeks that follow the New York Stock Exchange calendar. You play a gameweek by buying at least ${{playUsd}} inside it. Clubs are scored on who showed up, with one fixture a week from GW1.

How pick-up matching works

  • Matching starts inside your country, widens to your region after 24 hours in the queue, and to the whole world after 48 hours.
  • Clubs get a region name and a generated crest. A club never shows a member’s country, and the queue keeps only a salted hash of it, deleted once you are seated.
  • Two wallets funded by the same first sender are never seated in the same club.
  • You can opt out of matching at any time.

Gameweeks and Matchdays

A gameweek runs from the first NYSE session of the week to the last close of the week. Stock tokens trade around the clock on DEXs, so the rules say precisely which buys belong to which gameweek.

  • Buy window. A buy counts for the gameweek whose window it falls in. A window opens at the previous gameweek’s final close and shuts at its own final close. A buy on Saturday counts for the next gameweek.
  • Matchday. Standings are cut at the close of each trading day: 4:00 pm ET, or 1:00 pm ET on a half-day. Your club’s Matchday card is refreshed at each cut.
  • GW0 is the preseason. It opens with clubs on Tue Nov 24 2026 at 00:00 ET and has a table but no fixtures.
  • Season 0 runs from Nov 24 2026 to the close on Fri Jan 29 2027: GW0 to GW9.

The lineup rule

A member plays a gameweek only by making a qualifying buy of at least ${{playUsd}} inside that gameweek’s buy window. Everyone else is on the Bench. The rule is applied to the gameweek, so a buy last week does not make you a player this week.

What counts as a qualifying buy

A buy qualifies when all of these hold:

  • it was made through Tropa Club, so it carries the Builder Code tag;
  • it is worth at least ${{playUsd}} at the time of the buy;
  • it is not part of a wash pattern: a buy, then a sell, then another buy of the same token within 24 hours scores nothing, for either buy;
  • the wallet is not on the public exclusion list (founder and test wallets).

What the Matchday card shows

  • Only playing members count toward a fixture result and toward the 80% participation bonus.
  • Each Matchday card shows who has played, who is benched and a top scorer line: the playing member with the best gameweek return so far, shown as a percentage.
  • The Bench is free and always available. It is a seat on the team sheet. It is never described as a way to earn a perk or a prize.

Member return and the club score

A member’s gameweek return is the time-weighted percentage return of the stock tokens they bought through Tropa Club, measured over the gameweek’s window. Lots are matched first in, first out, and valued at the token’s mark at the cut: the Chainlink round at the cut, or else the stored DEX marks. A return is a percentage, never a dollar amount.

A club’s gameweek score is the median return of its playing members. The median is used on purpose: one member’s lucky pick or a larger buy cannot carry the club, and a larger bankroll earns no advantage. Stock clubs get no score.

Fixtures

Fixtures start in GW1 (Mon Nov 30 2026). They are played only between private and pick-up clubs that have at least three members when the fixtures are drawn. Public stock clubs never play fixtures.

Pairing

Clubs are grouped into size bands of 3 to 5, 6 to 10, 11 to 20 and 21 or more members. Within a band the pairing is a shuffle seeded by the gameweek and the band, so it is reproducible. If a band has an odd club, it moves up into the next band. The last club left gets a bye.

Call-ups

A playing member can tap a benched teammate on the Matchday card. That opens a ready-made WhatsApp or Telegram message: “we need you for the 80% bonus this week”. It is a nudge between friends and carries no prize. Limits: one call-up per teammate per gameweek from each caller, and call-ups are hidden wherever buying is unavailable.

The Supporters table

Public stock clubs are ranked in their own table on participation and streaks only. No returns appear on it, and no stock is ranked against another by price.

  1. Participation: the share of the club’s members who played the gameweek.
  2. Streaks: the share of members with a live payday streak.
  3. Ties go to the club with more members, then by name.

Payday streak

The payday streak is a personal habit badge. It does not change fixtures or tables.

  • One qualifying buy of ${{playUsd}} or more in any rolling 14 days keeps the streak alive.
  • Each calendar month has one free skip: a gap of up to 28 days survives if the month in which the missed 14-day deadline fell has not used its skip.
  • Same-day buys count once.

The streak is there for people who earn irregularly: a fixture asks for a buy this gameweek, but the streak only asks for a buy every fortnight or so.

Scouts

Every member has a personal invite link, tropaclub.xyz/j/ followed by a code. Club invite links a member sends carry the same code. A scout gets credit only when a friend really becomes a fan.

  • First touch. A new wallet, one with no verified buy and no other club yet, that arrives through the link is linked to that scout once, and the link never changes. You cannot scout yourself.
  • What counts. The friend’s first verified buy through Tropa Club after arriving, still held at ${{scouts.holdUsd}} or more of that token at D+7. Sign-ups and joins earn nothing.
  • Not the same money. No credit when the friend was funded by the scout, or both share a first funder (the first USDC or WETH sender in the 30 days before the check).
  • Limit. At most {{scouts.creditsPerGw}} credits per scout per gameweek, counted in the gameweek of the friend’s first buy.
  • Stamps. A Scout stamp on the ticket at {{scouts.scoutAt}} credited mate, a Captain’s armband at {{scouts.captainAt}}. The Top scouts table ranks scouts by credited mates who still hold ${{scouts.holdUsd}} or more.
  • Founders’ Pennant stamp. The first {{scouts.foundersLimit}} wallets whose first verified buy was still held at ${{scouts.holdUsd}} or more at D+7 get a Founders’ Pennant stamp on their ticket.

No prizes tied to returns

Returns can appear as a status on a Matchday card or in a fixture. Nothing of value depends on them. No prize, rebate, fee or access is given for a higher return, and the brand never uses win language, odds or jackpots around money. Fixtures compare the median of members who showed up, and the bonus rewards showing up.

Founders’ Pennant

The Founders’ Pennant is an onchain crest, an attestation on Base through the Ethereum Attestation Service, with no money in it. It goes to every member who played GW0 (Nov 24 to Nov 27) and still held the position seven days after their first buy. Every eligible member gets the same crest, with no ranking.

No partner perk is shown here or on the site until one is signed. If a partner perk is agreed, it is paid by the partner through its own tool to an eligibility list we publish, and Tropa Club never holds those funds. Crests for table positions in later gameweeks and seasons are planned, and they carry no money either.
