      Sun Nov 22 2026#

      • Cost-basis and P&L statement is live. Any holder can open a statement, adjusted for splits and stock dividends. Shipped Nov 22, four days ahead of the Nov 26 target.
      • The Ground and The Attendance open, read-only. Every stock token has a ground, every wallet with a balance has a seat, and every wallet can find its matchday ticket. Real fans and ghosts are counted by the Real Holders method.
      • The indexer now continues from the last block it applied instead of replaying, and is scheduled every six hours.
      • Public data feed. The league table, token seats, wallet shards and global stats are written as static JSON. See the schema.
      • The buy engine is complete. Quotes through an aggregator with Aerodrome first, the 1.5% guard with its units check, corporate-action holds, Builder Code suffix on every call, send strategies for batched and plain wallets, attribution verification, and plain-sentence errors. See Buying and safety.

      Sat Nov 21 2026#

      • Idea locked. A free investing club for your group chat on Coinbase’s stock tokens on Base: $10 a week, a gameweek lineup, fixtures, call-ups, no prizes on returns. The product is named Tropa Club, with the domain tropaclub.xyz and the X account @tropaclubxyz.
      • Builder Code registered on the Base dashboard: bc_vdp89s9b.
      • Brand. The Banner T mark, a capital T made from a crossbar and a swallowtail banner, with the Midnight, Marigold and Chalk palette.
      • First full count. All 21.8 million transfers of 90 minted stock tokens, Aug 12 to Nov 21, replayed to balances that match reported supply within 0.6%. It found 39,746 holders, 4,968 of them real fans of at least one token.
      • Buy path simulated on Base mainnet for NVDAc, SNDKc (multiplier 8) and TSLAc, with nothing spent.
