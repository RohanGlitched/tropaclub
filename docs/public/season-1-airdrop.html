      A planned token

      $TROPA has not launched. Caps are points: they can’t be bought, sold or transferred, and they are not a token. Nothing on this page is financial advice, an offer to sell or a promise of a token. The live page with your own Caps is tropaclub.xyz/season.

      How to earn Caps#

      Caps come only from verified onchain actions and a few self-reported social quests, at small fixed values. The amount bought plays no part: a $5 buy earns the same Caps as a $500 one, and returns decide nothing. The weekly values are the same rules as Points (Caps).

      Social quests#

      Three small quests on X, for at most 0.25 Caps a season. A signed-in wallet adds its X handle once, does a quest, then taps “I did this” on the quests section. The like-and-repost quest opens once the Season 1 post is up on @tropaclubxyz.

      1. Social Caps count only for wallets with at least one verified buy in Season 1. Until that first buy they are held as pending.
      2. A wallet enters its X handle once, signed in. One handle per wallet and one wallet per handle. The handle cannot be changed after the first social claim.
      3. Social quests are self-reported. We spot-check before the snapshot; claims we can't confirm are removed.
      4. Social Caps are capped at 0.25 per wallet for the whole season.

      Each quest card shows one of four statuses: open (not claimed), pending (claimed, waiting for the wallet’s first verified Season 1 buy), counted (in the ledger) or removed (a spot-check could not confirm it). A removed claim’s Caps are taken back out of the ledger on the next update.

      Checks and exclusions#

      Caps are meant for people who buy and hold, so farming them with many wallets doesn’t work. These checks run before anything counts, and again before the snapshot. The live page shows how many times each one has caught something, as counts only and never addresses.

      • Shared funders. No credit for a friend funded with ETH, USDC or WETH from the scout's wallet, or sharing a first sender (the first wallet to send it ETH, USDC or WETH) with the scout or with another of the scout's credited friends. The founder Cap uses the same idea: members funded from the founder’s wallet, or sharing a first funder with the founder or with each other, count once. Native ETH transfers are looked at too.
      • Wash trades. A buy, then a sell, then another buy of the same token within 24 hours scores nothing, for either buy.
      • Buys that don’t verify. Only buys made through Tropa Club that pass every check in what counts as a qualifying buy earn Caps. A buy not sent from the wallet itself, not through the pinned route, at a price off the mark, or where no stock token arrived earns nothing.
      • Team wallets. Founder, test and contest wallets are on a public exclusion list and get no allocation. The list is on the stats page.
      • New wallets are provisional. A wallet first seen less than 24 hours before its first verified buy, or one that cannot be dated yet, is held as provisional. Its Caps show on the table but are decided at the snapshot.
      • Social spot-checks. Social quests are self-reported. We spot-check before the snapshot; claims we can’t confirm are removed.
      • Duplicate X accounts. One X handle per wallet and one wallet per handle. Duplicate X accounts or wallets are removed and can disqualify the wallet.

      Airdrop terms#

      The terms of the planned $TROPA airdrop for Season 1, word for word as on tropaclub.xyz/season.

      1. $TROPA is a planned token. If it launches, Season 1 Caps convert to a share of the Season 1 airdrop pool, in proportion to Caps at the snapshot.
      2. There's no guarantee it launches, or that it will have any value. Not financial advice.
      3. Not available to US persons or in sanctioned regions; the same region rules apply to claims.
      4. Wallets that fail our checks (shared funders, wash trades, buys that don't verify) get no allocation. Team wallets are excluded.
      5. Caps themselves can't be bought, sold or transferred.
      6. Terms may be updated before the snapshot; changes are announced on @tropaclubxyz.

      Questions#

      Do I have to buy?#

      Yes. Caps come from verified buys and from credited friends, and social Caps count only once your wallet has a verified buy in Season 1.

      Does the amount I buy count?#

      No. Only playing and holding count, so a $5 buy counts the same as a $500 one.

      Can I farm with many wallets?#

      No. Friends who share a first funder with their scout or with each other are not credited, wash trades and buys that do not verify earn nothing, wallets created in the 24 hours before their first buy are held as provisional until the snapshot, and wallets that fail the checks get no allocation.

      Can I make extra X accounts?#

      Don't make extra X accounts or wallets; duplicates are removed and can disqualify the wallet.

      Anything else: DM @tropaclubxyz on X. Tropa Club will never message you first to ask for a signature or a payment.
